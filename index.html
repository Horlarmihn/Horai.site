Editorial Standard

AI can suggest. Humans verify.

Every claim on HORAI is checked before it's published. Not after.
01Unknown stays unknown. If something isn't confirmed, HORAI says so on screen.
02Estimates are labelled. A projected figure is never presented as a hard number.
03Incomplete data is disclosed. Partial statistics are shown as partial — not rounded into certainty.
04Unverified claims don't run. If it can't be checked, it doesn't publish as fact.

Behind every goal is a decision. Behind every decision is a pattern. Behind every pattern is a story — and behind every story is something waiting to be discovered.
